# about

this application serves to greatly simplify the process of ai (artificial intelligence) training and running, by formatting and oversimplifying the process to a set of easily managable GUI (graphical user interface, ie this application) tools that even a novice to AI can master, as long as they have access to sufficient hardware.

the point of such a project would be to bring power and agency back to the hands of any ordinary person, as long as that person possesses the hardware to run AI training and hosting. In other words, as long as the computational power is reasonable
