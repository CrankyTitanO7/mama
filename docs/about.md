# about
a short about page on the project's goals and aims

## introduction

this application serves to greatly simplify the process of ai (artificial intelligence) training and running, by formatting and oversimplifying the process to a set of easily managable GUI (graphical user interface, ie this application) tools that even a novice to AI can master, as long as they have access to sufficient hardware.

the point of such a project would be to bring power and agency back to the hands of any ordinary person, as long as that person possesses the hardware to run AI training and hosting. In other words, as long as the computational power is reasonable, any person can create and run any kind of model easily with no prior AI experience.


## AI slop and accountability

this application does rely heavily on AI generated code. While the morality of AI is grey, I will not pretend that I think AI is great, or that I had no choice in the matter. I considered only speed and coding ability, and as I am still a novice in programming, I left the bulk of the programming to the AI. 

I am, however, the bulk producer of all intellectual concepts presented in this application. I designed each component, workflow, page, and aesthetic in the Miro software extensively.

While the execution was done in a morally grey area of artificial intelligence, I can assure you that the initial seed and structure was all my original brainchild. Since AI cannot take proper accountability, I (or the developer) will take upon several duties, including writing this about page and verifying code before each version release.


## mission

I believe that in the advent of AI, what people really lacked was agency. The morally repulsive part of AI comes primarily from its destruction of the environment and excessive consumption of resources. 

What this application aims to do by simplifying the training and running process is to allow people to control exactly how much energy, resources, water, etc go into running and training ai. In other words, you know exactly how malignant your model(s) are to the environment and etc. Additionally, you train the model on data that you yourself curate (removing the ethics of corporate data-stealing) and perform your own upkeep (removing the ethics of ai upkeep in big organizations, such as energy consumption/waste).

while this does not solve all the problems of AI, by keeping this project open-source and easy, one day people will have the tools to at least explore viable options to mitigate the filth of slop.
