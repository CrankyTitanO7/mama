# about

## introduction

this application serves to greatly simplify the process of ai (artificial intelligence) training and running, by formatting and oversimplifying the process to a set of easily managable GUI (graphical user interface, ie this application) tools that even a novice to AI can master, as long as they have access to sufficient hardware.

the point of such a project would be to bring power and agency back to the hands of any ordinary person, as long as that person possesses the hardware to run AI training and hosting. In other words, as long as the computational power is reasonable, any person can create and run any kind of model easily with no prior AI experience.


## AI and accountability

this application does rely heavily on AI generated code. While the morality of AI is grey, I will not pretend that I think AI is great, or that I had no choice in the matter. I considered only speed and coding ability, and as I am still a novice in programming, I left the bulk of the programming to the AI. 

I am, however, the bulk producer of all intellectual concepts presented in this application. I designed each component, workflow, page, and aesthetic in the Miro software extensively.
